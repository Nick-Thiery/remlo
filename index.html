Remlo: free money tools for migrant workers in Singapore

Remlo helps you make everyday money decisions with clear information in your own language. It is free, and you can start as a guest without an account.

What you can do

  • Compare remittance estimates before you send money home
  • Plan a monthly budget and track savings goals
  • Learn to spot scams with a short quiz and official help links
  • Find salary, loan and emergency information

12 languages

English, தமிழ், हिंदी, বাংলা, မြန်မာ, සිංහල, Filipino, Bahasa Indonesia, 中文, ภาษาไทย, اردو, नेपाली

What Remlo does not do

Remlo does not transfer or hold money, connect to your bank account, or ask for bank passwords or OTPs. It offers general information and illustrative estimates, not financial advice.
